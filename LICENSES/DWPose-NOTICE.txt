Project modifications (2026):
- pose_architecture.py adapts CSPNeXt and RTMCC to a portable pure-PyTorch
  implementation without the MMPose/MMCV registry and runtime wrappers.
- The final keypoint channels select 73 annotated COCO-WholeBody landmarks;
  the shared MLP, GAU, and SimCC classifiers retain compatible pretrained
  structure. The smaller GAU context is subsequently fine-tuned.
- The complete model adds the project's training, lifting, metadata,
  checkpoint, and ONNX export components.
